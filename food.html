Building a Healthy Plate

Why a Balanced Meal Matters?

A healthy plate is an easy blueprint for a balanced meal. Fill half your plate with colorful fruits and vegetables for vitamins and fiber. Dedicate one-quarter to lean proteins like poultry, fish, or beans for muscle repair. Fill the final quarter with whole grains like quinoa or brown rice to sustain energy.

Learn more about healthy eating here: UC Davis Health Nutrition Guide

Smart Snack Choices

Healthy snacks provide steady energy and keep your metabolism active between meals. Choosing nutrient-dense whole foods like fruits, nuts, and vegetables prevents blood sugar crashes. Pairing complex carbohydrates with lean protein—such as apple slices with peanut butter or veggies with hummus—keeps you full and focused longer.

Nutrition Tips for Students

Drink Enough Water

Water helps the body stay active, supports digestion, and keeps the brain focused. Students should carry a water bottle and drink water throughout the day.

Eat Colourful Food

Different colours in fruits and vegetables give different nutrients. A colourful plate often means a healthier plate.

Read more about healthy food choices here: Nhs.uk

Quick Nutrition Note

Try this simple rule: fill half your plate with fruits and vegetables, one quarter with grains, and one quarter with protein.
